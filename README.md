# RetailPulse: Tableau Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

RetailPulse is an interactive Tableau dashboard built using the Sample Superstore dataset. The project analyzes sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional trends to generate actionable business insights.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to identify growth opportunities, optimize discount strategies, and monitor business performance across categories, regions, and customer segments.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze sales performance over time
- Identify profitable and loss-making product categories
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate regional sales performance
- Study the impact of discounts on profitability
- Highlight top-performing customers
- Measure contribution of different customer segments

---

## Dataset

**Dataset Used:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains:

- Orders
- Sales
- Profit
- Discounts
- Customer Information
- Product Categories
- Regional Data

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend

**Purpose:**
Track sales performance over time and identify seasonal patterns.

**Key Insight:**
Helps detect growth trends and sales fluctuations.

---

### 2. Sales by Category

**Purpose:**
Compare sales across product categories.

**Categories:**
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

---

### 3. Profit by Sub-Category

**Purpose:**
Identify the most and least profitable products.

**Business Value:**
Supports product portfolio optimization.

---

### 4. Regional Sales Map

**Purpose:**
Visualize sales distribution geographically.

**Business Value:**
Identify high-performing and underperforming regions.

---

### 5. Discount vs Profit Analysis

**Purpose:**
Understand the relationship between discounting and profitability.

**Business Value:**
Optimize discount strategies.

---

### 6. Top 5 Customers

**Purpose:**
Highlight highest-value customers.

**Business Value:**
Support customer retention initiatives.

---

### 7. Segment-wise Contribution

**Segments:**
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

**Business Value:**
Evaluate customer segment performance.
